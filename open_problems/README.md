# Open Problems

Copied and slightly clarified from Section 10 of the paper.
Each item is intentionally left open; closing any of them is a valuable contribution.

| ID | Statement | Status |
|----|-----------|--------|
| 1  | Formal proof of the trilemma with an explicit entropy definition | Open |
| 2  | Derivation of SDV_threshold from first principles | Open (empirical) |
| 3  | Convergence proof under Lipschitz continuous valence field V | Conjecture |
| 4  | Generalisation of the incremental Ξ and similarity updates for r > 1 | Open |
| 5  | Scaling laws relating grid size to physical task complexity | Open |
| 6  | Optimal T_decay for varying environmental dynamics | Open |
| 7  | Full FPGA synthesis and power measurement on the target platform | Open (empirical) |
| 8  | Formal sufficiency proof that the circular Ξ metric is adequate for stabilisation tasks | Open |
| 9  | Characterisation of the classes of tie-breaking policies τ that leave the attractor-basin structure of the full 4096-cell grid unchanged (cf. Open Problem 7 of the Arche Selector paper) | Open |

When you solve one of these, open a pull request that:

1. Adds a short proof or experimental report under `open_problems/solved/`
2. Updates the status table above
3. Updates the corresponding section of the paper (if you have write access) or opens an issue with the patch
